Delete your Out of Bounds account

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Who we are

Out of Bounds is a live golf scoring service operated by Ladies Love Golf Ltd.

How to delete your account

  1. In the app or at score.outofboundsevents.com: sign in, go to Settings, tap Delete my account. Type DELETE to confirm.
  2. By email: send a message to hello@outofboundsevents.com from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, photos, device tokens, any events you created and their scores. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

What is kept

  • Invoices for 6 years (UK tax law).
  • Anonymised aggregate statistics that cannot identify you.
