Questions

The things people ask on the first tee.

Does it do handicaps?

Yes. World Handicap System, with the playing handicap worked out from the tee's slope and rating, and the allowance set per format (85% for better ball, 95% for individual, or whatever the club uses).

Which formats?

Individual Stableford, pairs better ball and four-ball 2 from 4, with an optional second format shown from the same scores so you can run a team prize and an individual prize at once.

What if there's no signal on the course?

Scores save on the phone and send themselves when the signal comes back. The scorer sees a clear note that they're waiting, so nothing gets lost quietly.

What if the scorer's phone dies?

Anyone else in the group taps "take over scoring" and carries on. The hand-over is logged so the organiser can see it.

Do players need an account or an app?

No. A link or a QR code opens the scoring page in their phone's browser. Nothing to download, nothing to sign up for.

Can I brand it?

Yes. Your logo and colours on every screen, your sponsors on the telly. Licensees get their branding on every event they run.
