How it works

Three steps. That's it.

No training. No manuals. No IT department. Just create, share, and go.

1

Create your event

Enter your competition details, add players, and choose your format. Takes a couple of minutes.

2

Share the link

Each group gets a scoring link. Players open it on their phone — no app download, no account needed.

3

Watch scores come in live

Scores update in real time on the leaderboard. Put it on the big screen in the clubhouse.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Built specifically for the way golf events actually work.

Live Stableford scoring

Individual, better ball pairs, or best 2 from 4. All the formats you actually use, scored live as players go round.

Automatic handicap calculations

Slope, course rating, and playing handicap allowances all handled automatically. No spreadsheets needed.

Real-time leaderboard

A live leaderboard on every player's phone, plus a stunning big screen display for the clubhouse TV.

Custom branding

Add your logo and choose your colours. Make it look like yours, because it is.

Sponsor integration

Show sponsor logos and slides on the big screen leaderboard. Great for charity days and corporate events.

Works on any device

No app to download. No accounts for players. Just open the link in any phone browser and start scoring.
