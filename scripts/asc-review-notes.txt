Out of Bounds is a live golf scoring app for event organisers. Organisers sign in, set up a golf competition, and manage scoring on the day. Players do not need the app — they score on their own phones using a web link the organiser shares.

To sign in for review:
1. On the sign-in screen, tap "Sign in with email".
2. Enter: review@outofboundsevents.com
3. A code field appears. Enter: 479649

This signs you in to a demo organiser account (Fairway Events) with sample events and data.

Payments for event passes and subscriptions happen on the website (outofboundsevents.com), not inside the app. The app contains no in-app purchases, payment forms, or links to external payment pages.

Account deletion: Settings > Delete my account, or outofboundsevents.com/delete-account.
