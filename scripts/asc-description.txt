Out of Bounds is live golf scoring for any event, from a society day to a charity tournament. Set up your competition in minutes, share one link with your players, and watch the scores come in live.

How it works
1. Create your event: name, course, format, handicap allowance.
2. Add players and make the groups.
3. Go live. Players score on their own phones from the link you send. They never need an app.
4. Follow every group on the live leaderboard, and put it on the clubhouse screen.
5. Finish, and share the results on WhatsApp.

What you get
- Individual Stableford, best 2 from 4 and pairs better ball
- Playing handicaps worked out for you from each player's index
- A leaderboard that freezes at the hole you choose, so nobody knows who's won until the prizes
- Big screen for the clubhouse, controlled from your phone
- QR cards for the first tee
- Your logo and colours on every screen
- Prizes, results page and WhatsApp share
- Scorecard capture: photograph a card and we read the course
